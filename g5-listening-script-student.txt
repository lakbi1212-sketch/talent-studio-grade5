نص الاستماع · الصف الخامس · الاستعداد للامتحان النهائي
Listening Script · Grade 5 · Final Exam Preparation

هذا هو النص الكامل لما ستسمعه في تسجيل كل نموذج. اقرأه أثناء التدريب، وستُضاف التسجيلات الصوتية بصوت شموسة قريبًا.
This is the full text of what you will hear in each model's recording. Read it while you practise; the Shamousa-voice recordings are coming soon.


==================================================
النموذج الأول · Model 1
==================================================

الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
----------------------------------------

الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Boy: Are you going to watch the talent show on Thursday, Noor?
Girl: Of course! I'm singing in it. I practise my song every evening.
Boy: That's wonderful! Do you sing Arabic songs or English songs?
Girl: An English song. My music teacher helped me choose it.

الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Girl: What is Hamdan going to do in the talent show?
Boy: He's going to do some amazing tricks! He can make a coin disappear.
Girl: Really? That sounds difficult! Where did he learn that?
Boy: His uncle taught him. He practises every day after school.

الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: My brother is in the talent show too.
Boy: Oh really? What is he going to do?
Girl: He's going to play a famous Omani song. He practises every Friday with our grandfather.
Boy: Wow, the oud! I love its sound. I'll come early to hear him.

الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Boy: Did you see Aisha at practice yesterday, Salma?
Girl: Yes! She jumped and turned so beautifully. Everyone clapped.
Boy: She's very strong! How long has she been learning that?
Girl: For three years now. She goes to a sports club every week with her cousin.

الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Girl: What are the twins going to do in the show, Ali?
Boy: They're going to throw balls in the air and catch them!
Girl: How many balls can they use at the same time?
Boy: Three! Sometimes they drop one, and everyone laughs. It's very funny.

الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
----------------------------------------
Ms Maryam: Hello, Rashid! Are you ready for the school talent show next week?
Rashid: Hello, Ms Maryam! Yes, I am. I'm going to play the oud.
Ms Maryam: Wonderful! When do you practise?
Rashid: I practise every afternoon at home. My grandfather taught me to play.
Ms Maryam: That's lovely. And are you nervous about playing on stage?
Rashid: A little bit! But my friends will be there, so I feel happy.
Ms Maryam: Don't worry. Last year you played very well, and everyone loved it.
Rashid: Thank you! This year I will play a new song about the sea.
Ms Maryam: I can't wait to hear it. The show starts at nine o'clock on Thursday.
Rashid: Great! See you there, Ms Maryam.

النموذج الثاني · Model 2
==================================================

الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
----------------------------------------

الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Girl: How did people travel long ago, Grandpa?
Boy: My grandfather says people travelled on camels across the desert. The trips took many days.
Girl: That sounds very slow! Do people still do that today?
Boy: Only for fun now. Tourists ride camels at the beach.

الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Boy: Look at this photo of Muscat! Everything looks so tall and new.
Girl: Yes! My father works in a very tall building. It has twenty floors.
Boy: Twenty floors! How does he go up every day?
Girl: There's a fast lift. It takes only one minute to reach his office.

الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: What are you doing, Khalid?
Boy: I'm doing my homework on this tablet. All our school books are on it now.
Girl: That's great! Do you still use paper notebooks?
Boy: Sometimes, but the tablet is easier. I can also watch lessons on it.

الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Girl: Did you visit the old village yesterday, Ahmed?
Boy: Yes! We saw my grandfather's old house. It's made of mud and palm leaves.
Girl: Wow! Did people really live in houses like that?
Boy: Yes, long ago. The houses were small, but they were cool in summer.

الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Boy: How do you come to school every day, Maryam?
Girl: My mother drives me in our new car. It's white and very comfortable.
Boy: Nice! How long does the trip take?
Girl: Only ten minutes. In my grandmother's time, children walked for an hour!

الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
----------------------------------------
Yousuf: Grandpa, what was life like when you were a boy?
Salim: Oh, very different, Yousuf! We lived in a small village near the mountains.
Yousuf: Did you go to school?
Salim: Yes, but the school was far. I walked for an hour every morning with my friends.
Yousuf: An hour! And what did you do after school?
Salim: I helped my father with our goats. In the evening, we sat outside and told stories.
Yousuf: Did you have a television?
Salim: No, we didn't. We didn't even have electricity! We used lanterns at night.
Yousuf: Wow! Life was hard, Grandpa.
Salim: Maybe, but we were happy. Families were close, and the nights were full of stars.

النموذج الثالث · Model 3
==================================================

الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
----------------------------------------

الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Girl: Where did your class go last week, Said?
Boy: We went to Ras Al Jinz! We saw big green turtles on the beach at night.
Girl: Amazing! What were the turtles doing?
Boy: They were laying eggs in the warm sand. Our guide told us to be very quiet.

الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Boy: What are you doing this weekend, Huda?
Girl: My family is going to Jabal Shams. We're going to walk in the mountains.
Boy: That's exciting! Is it cold up there?
Girl: Yes, very cool and windy. I'm taking my jacket and my camera.

الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: You look happy today, Fahad!
Boy: I am! We went on a boat trip yesterday and saw dolphins jumping near us.
Girl: Wow! How many did you see?
Boy: About ten! They jumped very high out of the water. I took lots of photos of them with my new camera.

الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Boy: Did you enjoy the school trip, Lamya?
Girl: Yes, it was the best trip ever! We walked inside Al Hoota Cave.
Boy: Was it very dark inside the cave?
Girl: Very dark! But we all had torches with us. We saw beautiful rocks and a small cold lake.

الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Girl: Where is your father this weekend, Mohammed?
Boy: He took my brothers to the Empty Quarter. They're sleeping in tents tonight.
Girl: In the desert? Isn't it very cold at night?
Boy: Yes, but they have warm blankets. They'll watch the stars before they sleep.

الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
----------------------------------------
Aisha: Hi, Hamid! Did you hear about the school trip next month?
Hamid: No, I didn't! Where are we going?
Aisha: We're going to explore Wadi Shab. It's a beautiful valley with blue pools.
Hamid: That sounds wonderful! How will we get there?
Aisha: By bus. It takes two hours from school. Then we walk for half an hour.
Hamid: Is the walk difficult?
Aisha: A little, so we must wear good shoes and take hats. And we need to take water.
Hamid: Can we swim in the pools?
Aisha: Yes! The water is clean and cool. But we must stay with the teachers.
Hamid: I can't wait! I'm going to ask my parents tonight.
Aisha: Me too. It's going to be the best trip of the year!

النموذج الرابع · Model 4
==================================================

الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
----------------------------------------

الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Boy: Where are you going, Hala?
Girl: To the fruit shop. My mother wants some apples and bananas for lunch.
Boy: Can I come with you? I want to buy some oranges too.
Girl: Sure! The shop is near the mosque. The fruit there is always fresh.

الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Girl: Do you smell that wonderful smell, Sami?
Boy: Mmm, yes! Fresh bread from the bakery! Let's go in and buy some for our breakfast.
Girl: Good idea. How many loaves does your family need?
Boy: Three, please. And let's get two cakes for my little sister.

الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: It's your birthday tomorrow, Omar! What do you want?
Boy: I really want a remote-control car! I saw a red one in that shop.
Girl: The big red one in the window? It looks so fast!
Boy: Yes! My father promised to buy it for me after school today.

الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Boy: Where does your father go every Friday morning, Zahra?
Girl: He goes to the fish market very early. He buys hammour and kingfish.
Girl: Does he go alone?
Girl: No, we all go with him. Then we cook the fish together for lunch. It's delicious!

الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Boy: Why is your grandmother at the souq today, Amal?
Girl: She's buying spices. She needs cardamom and saffron for her famous biryani.
Boy: Does she go there every week?
Girl: Yes, every Thursday. She says the souq spices are the best in Oman.

الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
----------------------------------------
Mother: Muna, we need to go shopping this afternoon. Can you help me?
Muna: Of course, Mum! What do we need to buy?
Mother: First, we need rice, milk and eggs from the supermarket.
Muna: Can we get some juice too? The mango juice, please!
Mother: All right. Then we're going to the shoe shop. Your old shoes are too small.
Muna: Great! Can I choose white shoes this time?
Mother: Yes, if they have your size. After that, we'll visit Aunt Fatma's shop at the souq.
Muna: The abaya shop? Wonderful! I love the beautiful colours there.
Mother: Yes. And Aunt Fatma always gives you Omani sweets!
Muna: Let's go now, Mum! I'll get my bag.
