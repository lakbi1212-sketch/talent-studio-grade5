الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
----------------------------------------
الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Girl: Where did your class go last week, Said?
Boy: We went to Ras Al Jinz! We saw big green turtles on the beach at night.
Girl: Amazing! What were the turtles doing?
Boy: They were laying eggs in the warm sand. Our guide told us to be very quiet.
الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Boy: What are you doing this weekend, Huda?
Girl: My family is going to Jabal Shams. We're going to walk in the mountains.
Boy: That's exciting! Is it cold up there?
Girl: Yes, very cool and windy. I'm taking my jacket and my camera.
الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: You look happy today, Fahad!
Boy: I am! We went on a boat trip yesterday and saw dolphins jumping near us.
Girl: Wow! How many did you see?
Boy: About ten! They jumped very high out of the water. I took lots of photos of them with my new camera.
الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Boy: Did you enjoy the school trip, Lamya?
Girl: Yes, it was the best trip ever! We walked inside Al Hoota Cave.
Boy: Was it very dark inside the cave?
Girl: Very dark! But we all had torches with us. We saw beautiful rocks and a small cold lake.
الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Girl: Where is your father this weekend, Mohammed?
Boy: He took my brothers to the Empty Quarter. They're sleeping in tents tonight.
Girl: In the desert? Isn't it very cold at night?
Boy: Yes, but they have warm blankets. They'll watch the stars before they sleep.
الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
----------------------------------------
Aisha: Hi, Hamid! Did you hear about the school trip next month?
Hamid: No, I didn't! Where are we going?
Aisha: We're going to explore Wadi Shab. It's a beautiful valley with blue pools.
Hamid: That sounds wonderful! How will we get there?
Aisha: By bus. It takes two hours from school. Then we walk for half an hour.
Hamid: Is the walk difficult?
Aisha: A little, so we must wear good shoes and take hats. And we need to take water.
Hamid: Can we swim in the pools?
Aisha: Yes! The water is clean and cool. But we must stay with the teachers.
Hamid: I can't wait! I'm going to ask my parents tonight.
Aisha: Me too. It's going to be the best trip of the year!
