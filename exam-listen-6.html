📜 نص الاستماع · Listening script
الاستماع 1 · Listening 1 - خمسة حوارات قصيرة · Five short dialogues
الحوار 1 · Dialogue 1
Girl: How did your grandfather go to the souq long ago?
Boy: He rode his camel every Friday morning.
Girl: Wow! How long did the trip take?
Boy: About two hours. Now he drives his car in ten minutes!
الحوار 2 · Dialogue 2
Boy: What is that old house made of, Grandma?
Girl: Mud and palm leaves. My family lived in one like it.
Girl: Was it hot inside in summer?
Boy: No, it was cool. The thick walls kept the heat out.
الحوار 3 · Dialogue 3
Girl: Where did people buy food in the past?
Boy: They went to the souq. My grandfather still buys his dates there.
Girl: And where do you buy food now?
Boy: At the supermarket - it's near our house.
الحوار 4 · Dialogue 4
Boy: How did people see at night before electricity, Grandpa?
Girl: They used lanterns with oil. Look, here is my father's old lantern!
Girl: It's beautiful! Can I hold it?
Boy: Carefully, yes. It's very old.
الحوار 5 · Dialogue 5
Girl: How did fishermen travel long ago?
Boy: They sailed wooden dhows with the wind.
Girl: That sounds slow! How do they go now?
Boy: They use motor boats. Much faster, but the dhows are more beautiful.
الاستماع 2 · Listening 2 - حوار · Dialogue
Grandmother: Nasser, come and look at this old photo. This was my school!
Nasser: Really, Grandma? It's so small! How many students were there?
Grandmother: Only twenty. We all sat in one room.
Nasser: Did you have computers?
Grandmother: No, not even a television. We wrote on small black boards.
Nasser: And how did you go to school?
Grandmother: I walked with my friends. It took an hour, but we sang songs on the way.
Nasser: Were you happy there, Grandma?
Grandmother: Very happy. We loved our teacher, and we read stories under a big tree. School was different, but it was good.

Listening 1 5 marks

You are going to hear five short dialogues about old and new life in Oman. Match the pictures with the dialogues. You will hear each dialogue twice.

Listening 2 5 marks

You are going to hear a dialogue between a grandmother and her grandson, Nasser, about school long ago. For each item, choose the correct option. You will hear the interview twice.
